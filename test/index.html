How to test

  1. 1

    Join the testers group

    Use the Google account your phone uses for Google Play. You only do this once: it covers every game and app here. On a phone, Google may ask you to open its desktop site before it shows the Join button.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Pick a game and opt in

    Choose a game, tap Opt in, then Become a tester on the page that opens.

    Opt in: Single Shot
  3. 3

    Install it from Google Play

    Tap Install, then keep it for two weeks and open it now and then. That's what lets it launch for everyone.

    Install: Single Shot

Good to know

Which phones can test?

Android phones and tablets with Google Play. Testing isn't open on iPhone or iPad.

Do I have to join the group for every game?

No. Join once and you can opt in to any game or app on this page, now and later.

The opt-in page says the test isn't available

Google can take a few minutes to see a new member of the group. Wait a little and open it again, signed in with the same Google account you joined with.

What does "Coming soon" mean?

That game isn't on Google Play yet. Join the group now, and you can opt in the day its test opens. Many of them can already be played in your browser from their page.

Do I have to pay?

No. Everything is free to play, just as it will be at launch, and test purchases are not charged.

What happens at launch?

Nothing changes for you. The game updates through Google Play, and your progress stays.

How do I send feedback?

Write to support@theramlakhan.com with your phone model and what happened. A level that felt unfair, a button that confused you, a bug: all of it helps.

Can I stop testing?

Any time. Open the game's Opt in link again and leave the program, or uninstall it.
