About the game

Ringburst is a one-touch neon arcade. You are a glowing core at the centre of the screen, and simple shapes creep in from every edge.

Hold anywhere and a ring grows from your core. Let go, and it bursts outward as a shockwave. Release while a shape sits right on the ring's edge for a PERFECT.
