About the game

Single Shot is a calm ricochet puzzle. Drag to aim, let go, and watch your orb bank off the walls. Pop every target in the room with that one shot.

Miss, and the room resets right away. Your last path stays faintly on screen, so you can see what went wrong and adjust. Clear it on the first try for three stars.

What's inside

One shot per room

Every room can be cleared with a single shot, and a solver checks each one.

Nine ideas, one at a time

Glass panes, gates, sliding platforms, pivots, portals, prisms and sparks, each with its own tutorial room.

A steady climb

40 rooms per chapter, from one bounce to eight.

Hints that show the way

Three free hints a day. A hint shows the exact path.

Share your shot

Save or share a picture of your clear and ask friends to match it.

No account
