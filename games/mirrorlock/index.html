About the game

Mirrorlock is a calm escape puzzle with a twist you can see at a glance: every piece has a mirror twin across a glowing line, and tapping either one moves both.

If anything stands in either twin's way, both bump back and you lose a heart. Read the board, find the pair that's free, and clear every piece. Do it with no bumps for three stars.
