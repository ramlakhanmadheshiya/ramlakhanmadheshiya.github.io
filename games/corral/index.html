About the game

Corral is a calm, colourful block puzzle with a twist: you don't clear lines, you fence in space.

Drag wall pieces onto a 9 × 9 field. When your walls close off a patch of empty cells, the pen fills with colour like a paint bucket, scores its area squared, and the walls around it vanish. Close a small pen now, or hold out for a big one?
