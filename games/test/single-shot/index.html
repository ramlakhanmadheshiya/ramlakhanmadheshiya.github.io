Testing on Google Play

Test Single Shot

A calm ricochet puzzle: drag to aim, bank one orb off the walls and pop every target in a single shot. Play it before everyone else, free.

Three steps

  1. Join the testers group

    Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your Android phone. On a phone, Google may ask you to open the desktop site to see the Join button.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in to Single Shot

    Open the link, then tap Become a tester.

    Opt in

  3. Install and keep it for 14 days

    Install from Google Play and play now and then. Keeping it for two weeks is what lets the game launch for everyone.

    Install from Google Play

Questions

Which phones work?

Android phones and tablets with Google Play. iPhone and iPad aren't supported yet.

The opt-in page says the test isn't available

Wait a few minutes after joining the group, then open the Opt in link again while signed in with the same Google account.

Does it cost anything?

No. Testing is free, and test purchases are not charged. When the game launches, it simply updates through Google Play and your progress stays.

How do I stop testing?

Open the Opt in link again and leave the program, or uninstall the game.

How do I send feedback?

Email support@theramlakhan.com with your phone model and what happened. Screenshots help.
