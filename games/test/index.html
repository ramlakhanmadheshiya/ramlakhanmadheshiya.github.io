Testers wanted

Play early

Every new game spends a few weeks in testing on Google Play before it launches. Testers get it first, for free, and help make it better. It takes about a minute.

Three steps

  1. Join the testers group

    Use the Google account you use for Google Play on your Android phone. On a phone, Google may ask you to open the desktop site to see the Join button.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in to a game

    Pick a game, tap Opt in, then Become a tester on the page that opens.

    Opt in: Single Shot

  3. Install it from Google Play

    Tap Install, then keep the game for two weeks and play it now and then. That's what lets it launch for everyone.

    Install: Single Shot

Good to know

Which phones can test?

Android phones and tablets with Google Play. iPhone and iPad aren't supported yet.

The opt-in page says the test isn't available

Wait a few minutes after joining the group, then open the Opt in link again while signed in with the same Google account.

Do I have to pay?

No. The games are free to play, and test purchases are not charged.

What happens at launch?

Nothing changes for you. The game updates through Google Play and your progress stays.

How do I send feedback?

Email support@theramlakhan.com with your phone model and what happened. Screenshots help.

Can I stop testing?

Yes, any time: open the Opt in link again and leave the program, or uninstall the game.
