About the game

Word Lantern is a calm word-light puzzle. Every tile in the grid belongs to one hidden word on the level's theme. Drag through neighbouring letters to find each word, and it lights up like a string of paper lanterns.

Light every tile to clear the level. Every answer fits in exactly one place, so each level has one clean solution.

What's inside

Fill-the-grid word finding

Each level is a theme, like Fruit, Weather or Castle, and a grid covered by its hidden words.

Shadows and beacons

Shadow tiles hide their letters until a word next to them is found. Beacons light a whole row and column.

A smooth climb

Grids grow one step at a time, from 3×3 to 8×7.

A new lantern every day

The same daily puzzle for everyone, harder as the week goes on.

Letter and word hints

The first letter hint each day is free.

No account

Progress stays on your device.
